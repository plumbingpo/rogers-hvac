Safety

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe, comfortable conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can result in carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. Rogers HVAC provides commercial installations and inspections that cover heat exchanger integrity, proper venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge verification, condensate drainage protection, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

The paperwork matters as much as the repair. We keep a service binder with detailed service logs and temperature and filter records, so you can demonstrate due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Heavy-use equipment, in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms, is put on a more frequent visit schedule.

Preventive Care

Scheduled HVAC Maintenance for Commercial Properties

Stopping failures before they happen is the most cost-effective way to manage commercial HVAC equipment. Every Rogers HVAC program is tailored to the property's equipment, usage, and budget, and covers scheduled inspections, coil cleaning, condensate line clearing, filter changes using the right MERV rating for the space, belt and bearing checks, refrigerant and static pressure testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, and seasonal preparation for peak demand.

Scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction of an emergency replacement and significantly reduces the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Our commercial clients consistently report fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower energy bills after implementing a program.
